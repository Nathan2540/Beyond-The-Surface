MEET THE FOUNDER

Faith Herbert

Environmental science and resource management graduate, development professional and advocate with more than 10 years of experience across the development space.

Her work and interests span health, young people, gender equality, social development, advocacy and community engagement. Faith has seen how information — when communicated simply, creatively and relatably — can help people understand their realities and make more informed decisions.

She founded Beyond the Surface from a desire to create a platform where important conversations can go deeper, information can become more accessible, and storytelling can educate, inspire and spark curiosity.
